# HARC Solutions

**Operations platform behind London’s trusted cleaning company — and a blueprint for service businesses.**

HARC is both a **live commercial cleaning brand** serving Greater London and a **full-stack operations system**: quotes, bookings, admin, staff, estate agents, payments, notifications, maps, and native iOS.

> Live: [harcsolutions.com](https://harcsolutions.com) · App: [app.harcsolutions.com](https://app.harcsolutions.com)

---

## Two layers, one product

| Layer | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand** | #1-rated positioning, end-of-tenancy and commercial cleaning across London |
| **Operating system** | Quote → book → schedule → execute → report → bill — with role portals |

Built with TUXX / Mustard Seed Group as a serious service-operations product — not a brochure site with a form.

## Public brand highlights

- **10+ years** experience narrative; **30+** partner agencies  
- Services: end of tenancy, light cleans, after-build, office, and specialist jobs  
- Instant quote flows + AI-assisted quoting on the marketing surface  
- Fully insured / DBS-checked positioning; London-wide coverage  

## Platform capabilities

```text
Marketing & Quote          Admin & Ops              Field & Partners
─────────────────          ───────────              ────────────────
Instant quote wizard  →    Scheduling & calendar →  Staff execution
AI quote assist            Job lifecycle            Route / maps
Service catalogue          CRM & clients            Reporting
Payments checkout          Work orders              Estate-agent portal
                           Messaging & notifs       Native iOS app
```

### Role-aware portals

- **Client** — quote, book, pay, track  
- **Admin / super-admin** — scheduling, CRM, CMS, settings, catalogues  
- **Staff** — execution, reporting, field flows  
- **Estate agent** — work-order conversion into jobs  

### Systems depth (public-safe)

- Edge workflows for quotes, payments, invites, follow-ups, AI assistants  
- Maps / geocoding for operational routing  
- Notification and email lifecycle (payment links, abandoned checkout, follow-ups)  
- Production CI gates, smoke checks, and App Store release track for iOS  
- Blog / CMS wired toward the marketing site  

## Outcomes the system optimises for

- Faster quote-to-book  
- Less admin between office and field  
- Partner agencies converting work orders cleanly  
- Reliable payments and follow-up  
- A native app that matches the web ops brain  

## Stack (high level)

- Modern TypeScript web app (admin + marketing + portals)  
- Native iOS companion  
- Managed backend with RLS-style multi-role security posture  
- Payments, email, and maps integrations  
- Cloudflare-oriented web delivery for public surfaces
